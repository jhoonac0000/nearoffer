    What is NearOffer?

    NearOffer is a local discovery platform for Faisalabad, Pakistan. It brings together offers from physical shops, booking information for wedding halls and marquees, a nearby masjid finder powered by Google Maps, and practical local guides across 24 categories. Customers use NearOffer to discover a place, review its published offer or information, and then visit or contact the business directly. NearOffer does not process payments or provide delivery through the platform.

    What is NearOffer?

    NearOffer is a local discovery platform for physical shops, venues and places in Faisalabad. It helps customers find offers and useful local information before visiting a business in person.

    Which city is NearOffer launching in?

    NearOffer is launching first in Faisalabad, Pakistan. Other cities are planned for later stages after the Faisalabad launch.

    How can a shop join NearOffer?

    A shop joins by completing the Dukaan Portal registration form on NearOffer. The NearOffer team reviews the submission, and verification is based on phone or WhatsApp confirmation plus a matching shop photo and address.

    Is NearOffer ranking for sale?

    No. NearOffer ranking is never sold; it is based on followers, genuine visits, ratings and new offers.

    How do customers use NearOffer?

    Customers browse categories and areas, open a shop or place, check its offer or information, and then visit or contact the business directly. NearOffer does not process payments or provide delivery through the platform.
